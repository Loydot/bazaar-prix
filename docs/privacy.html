Privacy Policy — Bazaar

Last updated: 26 August 2026

Bazaar is an Overwolf app that shows Warframe market prices, ranked by what items actually earn. This page explains what it does with data. The short version: the developer collects nothing about you, and nothing leaves your computer except what you choose to send to warframe.market by signing in.

What Bazaar does not do

  • It does not read the game's memory, inject code into it, or open or modify any Warframe file.
  • It asks for no account at all to browse prices, your inventory or drop tables. Only posting items for sale needs one — your warframe.market account — and that part is optional: see below.
  • It has no analytics, no telemetry and no crash reporting.
  • It does not send anything to a server owned by the developer. There is no such server.

What leaves your computer

Bazaar makes HTTPS requests to three places, and only to fetch public information:

  • loydot.github.io — a single file holding the price of every tracked item, refreshed every six hours. The app downloads it and reads it locally.
  • api.warframe.market — public prices and public sell listings, when you open an item to see current sellers.
  • api.warframestat.us and drops.warframestat.us — drop tables, to show where an item comes from.

These requests contain no information about you. Like any request on the internet they carry your IP address and standard browser headers, which those services handle under their own policies.

If you sign in to warframe.market

Bazaar can post and edit your sell listings on warframe.market. This is optional: as long as you do not sign in, nothing in this section applies.

  • Your email address and password are sent to warframe.market, and to nobody else, for the duration of the sign-in. The password is never stored, in any form.
  • In return the site issues a session token. That token is kept on your computer, in the app's local storage, so you do not have to sign in again every time. It is only ever sent to warframe.market.
  • Signing out erases the token straight away. So does uninstalling the app.
  • What happens to your listings after that is between you and warframe.market, under their own rules.

Your inventory

If you turn on inventory reading, the list of your items comes from Overwolf, which receives it from the game. Bazaar puts a price on each item and adds it up. That list stays on your computer: it is not sent anywhere, and the developer has no access to it.

What stays on your computer

  • Your language choice and whether you have seen the welcome screen, in the app's local storage.
  • A copy of the price list and the time it was taken, in the app's IndexedDB database, so the app opens instantly and still works offline.
  • If you sign in to warframe.market, the session token and your in-game name, in local storage.
  • If you turn on the inventory, the list of your items and its date, in the same database.

None of it ever leaves the machine. Uninstalling the app removes all of it.

Overwolf and advertising

Bazaar runs on the Overwolf platform, which has its own data practices, described in the Overwolf Platform Privacy Policy. The app reserves an area for advertising served by Overwolf; where ads are shown, Overwolf and its advertising partners collect data as set out in that policy. The developer of Bazaar receives none of it.

Children

Bazaar is not aimed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone, whatever their age.

Your rights

Nothing about you is collected or stored anywhere outside your own computer, so there is nothing to request, correct or delete from the developer. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling it removes everything it kept locally, session token included. For the warframe.market account itself, contact warframe.market.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date at the top.

Contact

Questions or concerns: helpers.bazaar@outlook.com. You can also open an issue on the project repository or ask on the Discord.
