Privacy Policy — Bazaar

Last updated: 26 August 2026

Bazaar is an Overwolf app that shows Warframe market prices, ranked by what items actually earn. This page explains what it does with data. The short version: it does not collect anything about you.

What Bazaar does not do

  • It does not read the game's memory, inject code into it, or open or modify any Warframe file.
  • It does not ask for an account, a name, an email address or a password.
  • It has no analytics, no telemetry and no crash reporting.
  • It does not send anything to a server owned by the developer. There is no such server.

What leaves your computer

Bazaar makes HTTPS requests to three places, and only to fetch public information:

  • loydot.github.io — a single file holding the price of every tracked item, refreshed every six hours. The app downloads it and reads it locally.
  • api.warframe.market — public prices and public sell listings, when you open an item to see current sellers.
  • api.warframestat.us and drops.warframestat.us — drop tables, to show where an item comes from.

These requests contain no information about you. Like any request on the internet they carry your IP address and standard browser headers, which those services handle under their own policies.

What stays on your computer

  • Your language choice and whether you have seen the welcome screen, in the app's local storage.
  • A copy of the price list and the time it was taken, in the app's IndexedDB database, so the app opens instantly and still works offline.

None of it ever leaves the machine. Uninstalling the app removes all of it.

Overwolf and advertising

Bazaar runs on the Overwolf platform, which has its own data practices, described in the Overwolf Platform Privacy Policy. The app reserves an area for advertising served by Overwolf; where ads are shown, Overwolf and its advertising partners collect data as set out in that policy. The developer of Bazaar receives none of it.

Children

Bazaar is not aimed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone, whatever their age.

Your rights

Since nothing about you is collected or stored anywhere outside your own computer, there is nothing to request, correct or delete. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling it removes everything it kept locally.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date at the top.

Contact

Questions or concerns: helpers.bazaar@outlook.com. You can also open an issue on the project repository or ask on the Discord.

Bazaar is an independent project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Digital Extremes, Warframe, warframe.market or Overwolf.
