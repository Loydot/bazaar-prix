Bazaar — Support

The Warframe market, ranked by what items actually earn.

Something not working, or a question the answers below don't cover? The fastest way is the Discord. You can also open an issue. Either way, say what you were doing. It gets read.

Questions people ask

Why is a price different from what I see in game chat?

Bazaar shows the median of sales actually concluded over the last 48 hours, not what people are asking for. Someone shouting a high price in chat has not sold anything yet. The median is what the item really changes hands for.

What does the Volume column mean?

Price multiplied by how many sell per day. It is the whole point of the app: an item asking 200p that moves twice a week earns less than a 40p mod that moves eighty times. Sorting by Volume puts the genuinely profitable items on top.

How often do prices update?

A single machine collects the entire market every six hours and publishes one file. Your copy downloads it when it starts and then every thirty minutes. Anything you click on is queried live, so an item you open is always current.

Baro Ki'Teer or Maroo's Bazaar is empty

That is normal when the trader is not in game. Their stock only exists while they are visiting. Baro shows up every two weeks; Varzia's rotation changes monthly.

An item is missing

Bazaar lists what warframe.market lists. If an item cannot be traded between players, it is not there. Newly released items appear once the market adds them, usually within a day or two.

Alt + B does nothing in game

Check the hotkey actually assigned: open the settings (the gear, top right), the in-game hotkey section shows the current binding. Another app may have taken the same combination. Change it from there.

The overlay only exists while Warframe is running. On the desktop, the main window is the one to use.

Prices look old and the dot next to them is grey

That means the last download failed and you are seeing the prices from before. Click Refresh. If it keeps failing, your connection or GitHub is the likely cause; the app keeps working with what it already has.

Does Bazaar read my game?

No. It does not read memory, inject anything, or open any Warframe file. The privacy policy spells out exactly what it does and does not do.

Where do the prices come from?

From warframe.market, whose community makes them. Drop sources come from warframestat.us. The collector that gathers them is open source.

If something is broken

  • Close and reopen the app. Prices are cached, so it costs you nothing.
  • Click Refresh to force a new download.
  • Still stuck? ask on the Discord, or open an issue, with what you did and what happened.
